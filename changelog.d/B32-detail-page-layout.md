### Changed

- The detail page is rebuilt into one flat one-screen flow: the same-row member
  circles move into the top bar and spread across its width, one mapping row
  replaces the duplicated large glyph and the star row, the mouth slot shrinks
  to a small card beside the larger writing card, the pronunciation control
  becomes an inline line under the writing card, and the 2×2 example cards
  stretch to the screen bottom.
- The detail page follows the notation selected on the chart page — the mapping
  row, the top bar and the pronunciation line all show that notation's symbol,
  and KK stays the default.
- Each example card shows one image and either one word or one sentence as the
  phoneme's display form decides; the two never render together.
- The handwriting entry moves from the top bar to a round button on the content
  layer's top-right corner and opens the same non-scored handwriting overlay.
- The 48 handwritten skeletons are redrawn so each is recognisable as its
  symbol and matches the printed letterform, and they are notation-aware: the
  phonemes whose three notations share one letterform share one skeleton, the
  pure long vowels take the extra long-vowel stroke, and the phonemes whose
  letterform itself differs switch skeleton with the notation.
- The handwritten model is drawn with an equal-ratio mapping centred in the
  writing card, so the letterform keeps its proportions in a wide card.
