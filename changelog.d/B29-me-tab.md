# B29 — Me tab: four pages, the review chain, settings and the About source line

- The Me tab grows into its four pages (REQ-032): the home is rebuilt as
  the mastery statistics card plus the review status strip (due count,
  hidden when none is due) and three drill-down entries to review,
  settings and about.
- The review drill-down (REQ-034) lists the due queue in due order with
  the fixed review-empty state and starts the shared quiz page over the
  review source; the chart status strip deep-links straight into it.
- Settings (SDD §3.8): the language switch applies zh-CN/en-US to all
  copy immediately through the new app-wide copy table, and the audio
  toggle keeps the pronunciation button and listening tasks visible with
  playback as a fixed-notice no-op when off.
- The About page (REQ-001) carries the version, the single source label
  with the ISBN slot reserved in the data structure (contingency
  public-tables labelling until licensed), the oss note, feedback and
  the device-only disclosure.
